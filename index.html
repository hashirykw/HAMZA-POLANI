How he teaches

Stories that stick. Answers that score.

Students call his classes fun — but every story is aimed at the mark scheme. History is taught in date order, geography theme by theme, and every answer is trained the way Cambridge awards marks.

  • Topic by topic, in order. Paper 1 from the Mughal decline to 1999; Paper 2 from land and climate to population.
  • Every question type, drilled. Source-based, 4-mark, 7-mark and 14-mark "how far do you agree" answers.
  • Timed past papers, marked properly. Checked against Cambridge mark schemes, with dates, names and statistics in every answer.
