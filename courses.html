O Level 2059 · IGCSE 0448

The two papers.

IGCSE 0448 shares the same core content and exam technique, so both are taught side by side.

01

History & Culture of Pakistan

Section A: one compulsory source-based question (25 marks). Section B: answer two of four questions (50 marks).

1 h 30 min75 marks50% of grade
02

Environment of Pakistan

Answer three of five questions, working with maps, graphs and photographs.

1 h 30 min75 marks50% of grade

Exam technique

Four question types. All practised.

Answers are trained on how Cambridge awards marks — backed by specific dates, names, statistics and places.

SourceSource-based

Reading and using sources — the compulsory Section A question on Paper 1.

44-mark describe

Short, precise description with the right facts.

77-mark explain

Explaining reasons with developed points.

1414-mark evaluate

"How far do you agree?" — balanced argument and a judgement.

FAQ

Questions students ask first.

Still unsure? The chat assistant in the corner answers in seconds, or message Sir Hamza directly.

Can I study O Level Pakistan Studies online?

Yes. Live, interactive online classes cover both Paper 1 (History & Culture) and Paper 2 (Environment of Pakistan) for students across Pakistan and abroad.

Is “Pak Studies” the same as Pakistan Studies?

Yes — Pak Studies, Pakistan Studies and PST all refer to the same subject: Cambridge O Level 2059 (and IGCSE 0448).

How many papers are there?

Two. Each paper is 1 hour 30 minutes, carries 75 marks and is worth 50% of the final grade.

How do I get an A* in Pakistan Studies?

Cover the full syllabus, practise every question type (source-based, 4, 7 and 14-mark), support answers with specific dates, names, statistics and places, and complete timed past papers marked against Cambridge mark schemes.

When should I start?

Most students study Pakistan Studies over two years — O1 and O2. You can join at any point; the free class is the easiest way to see where you stand.

Do you teach IGCSE Pakistan Studies too?

Yes. Classes cover both O Level 2059 and IGCSE 0448, which share very similar content.

Is there a free trial class?

Yes — your first class is free. Book through the contact page or message Sir Hamza on WhatsApp at +92 331 2364114.

Where are the on-campus classes?

On-campus classes are in Karachi (Bahadurabad area). Students from DHA, Clifton, PECHS, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, North Nazimabad, Gulistan-e-Jauhar and Bahria Town often join online to skip the commute.
