The teacher behind 6,000+ A* and A grades.

Sir Hamza Ali Polani — known to students as HMA, Hamza M. Ali — teaches Cambridge O Level and IGCSE Pakistan Studies in Karachi and online across Pakistan.

In his words

“My goal is to promote education and ensure every student is well-prepared to face future challenges.”

His mission, as he puts it: to polish your skills and guide you to academic excellence. His classes mix focused Pakistan Studies preparation with discussion, stories and the occasional breakfast with his students.
